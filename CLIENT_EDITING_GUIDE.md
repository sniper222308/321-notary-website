# Editing the 321 Notary website

1. Open https://app.pagescms.org/ and sign in with the email address used for your invitation. Open the 321 Notary website when it appears.
2. Choose a section from the menu. To change hours, open **Business Hours**, find the day, and switch **Closed** on or off. Enter opening and closing times in 24-hour format: 9:00 AM is `09:00`, 5:30 PM is `17:30`. Leave times in place on closed days.
3. To change a service, open **Services** and edit its name or description. Use the controls to add or remove services.
4. To replace the large landscape photo, open **Photos**, select the image and upload or choose a wide photograph of Kinzua Reservoir. To add your own photo in the About section, open **About** and choose an owner photograph. Leaving it empty shows the business badge.
5. For a temporary closure, open **Announcements**, enter a short message, and turn on **Show announcement**. Turn it off when the notice is no longer needed.
6. Click **Save** after editing each section. Saving sends the change for publication automatically. Uploading an image and saving the section that uses it may be separate steps.
7. Wait a few minutes, then refresh the live website and check your changes. If they do not appear, contact the person who manages the website.

To edit questions, open **Frequently Asked Questions**. You can add, remove, and reorder them. Words in braces such as `{hours}` automatically insert current information; keep them where useful.
